Product

An AI receptionist for your website.

FrontDesk AI chats with people who visit your site. It answers from your own knowledge base — services, prices, hours and FAQs — and books appointments into free slots within your opening hours. You share a receptionist link from Settings. Every booking, and the conversation behind it, appears in your admin dashboard.

Actual admin demo dashboard · Lumen Dental Studio · fictional demonstration data

How it works

From the first question to a booking you can see.

01

A visitor asks

They open your chat page and ask about your services, prices or hours, or request an appointment.

02

The AI answers and books

It replies from your knowledge base. When they give their name, phone, service and preferred time, it books a free slot of the length you set in Settings. The appointment always ends before you close, and closed days, holidays, overlaps and duplicate phone numbers are refused.

03

You see it in the dashboard

The appointment lands in your admin workspace. You can search, filter, and mark it confirmed, completed or cancelled, and open the original conversation when you need context.

Features

What you can actually do with it.

Every item below is in the app today. There is no website embed snippet, and the app does not send email or text-message notifications.

Open admin workspace →
01

Answers from your knowledge

The receptionist replies using the services, prices, hours and FAQs you keep in your knowledge base — not generic guesses.

02

Booking that respects your hours

Set appointment length in Settings (5–480 minutes). Bookings go only into free slots and must end by closing time. Closed weekdays, holidays, overlaps and a second booking on the same phone number are refused.

03

Collects the details you need

To book, the chat collects the visitor’s name, phone number, service and preferred date and time. A “Required info” button on the chat page explains this to them.

04

A dashboard for every booking

See today’s and upcoming appointments, conversation activity and booking conversion. Search and filter bookings, update their status, and jump to the original chat.

05

A knowledge base you edit

Add, edit, categorise or turn off answers whenever your services or prices change, so the receptionist stays aligned with your business.

06

Your name and logo, everywhere

Set your business name and upload a logo in Settings. It appears in the admin, the landing page, the chat header and next to every assistant reply.

07

A chat that fits the screen

The visitor chat is built for phones and desktops. On a larger screen, visitors can drag the corner to resize the window. Share your receptionist link from Settings — it is a page on your site, not a third-party widget.

08

Your server, your AI key, your data

Install it on your own computer or VPS. Use your OpenAI-compatible API key (OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, Together or a local Ollama server); the key is encrypted at rest. Admin access uses signed sessions, CSRF protection, bcrypt passwords and rate limits. Records stay scoped to your business.
